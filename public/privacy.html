Apple67 privacy

Updated 17 September 2026. Apple67 is a supper-planning pilot. Only share locations and names you are comfortable sharing with people who have your invitation link.

Plans, participant names, starting/return locations, transport choices, venue references, votes and manually entered bill information are stored using Supabase. Your browser stores the current draft and access capabilities. Keep invitation links and host access private. Shared-plan access expires according to the plan settings; expiry does not promise immediate database deletion. Hosts can close plans or use available deletion controls.

Google restaurant choices are saved as place IDs. Google names, ratings, addresses, opening information and coordinates are held only in active-session memory, not saved in our plan records. Restaurant searches send the selected search centre (which may be the average of participant locations) to Google. Google receives restaurant IDs when details are requested. Google Routes receives journey coordinates and requested times for transit planning. LTA receives stop/service queries for schedule and bus-arrival checks. Opening a ride-app link sends the displayed pickup/final destination to that app where supported; intermediate stops may require manual entry. OneMap receives search queries and journey coordinates/times to provide Singapore location and routing results.

Supabase and Cloudflare operate the backend and hosting and may process technical access logs. The restaurant service stores request counters and short-lived hashed connection buckets to limit abuse, and records outcome and duration without personal addresses or credentials. We do not perform automatic payment verification.

Google processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy. See also the OneMap website. Clearing this site's browser data removes your local copies and may remove access to your plan; it does not delete shared server records.

Contact the project through GitHub without posting personal locations, invitation capabilities or private keys publicly.

Restaurant swipe ratings (like, pass or neutral) and the shared shortlist are stored with the plan. Participants with its invitation can see shared ratings and progress. Photo requests send a Google photo reference to Google; image delivery also contacts Google’s image service. Review requests send the restaurant ID. Photo URLs, photographer credits and review text are used for active display only, not saved in plan records.

Choosing or taking a receipt photo automatically sends it through a Supabase function to Google Gemini for extraction, as noted beside the photo buttons. Remove personal, confidential and payment details before using AI. This scanner uses the paid Gemini API: Google does not use paid-service prompts/responses to improve its products, but may retain them temporarily for abuse detection and required legal disclosures. Apple67 does not save receipt photos or raw AI results in its database or application logs; this does not override Google’s retention policies. Only confirmed bill items use existing local/shared bill storage. See Gemini API terms.

Party invitations use open name selection: anyone with the party link or code can select an existing guest name and access that guest’s votes, bill claims and manual payment status. Share invites only with people you trust. Host controls require a separate browser-stored capability and are never included in invite links. Returning guests are remembered using site storage, not IP addresses; private browsing or clearing site data requires choosing a name again.

Home addresses are remembered in this browser under your chosen name so you can reuse them in another party. A new party offers “Use saved home”; choosing it shares the address with that party. Change or forget the saved home from the address controls. Forgetting the browser copy does not remove an address already shared with a party. People using the same name in the same browser share this saved preference; it is not an account or IP-based identity.
